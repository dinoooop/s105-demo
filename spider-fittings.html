Technical Data

Materials, Load Capacity & Selection

Recommended load values are for model selection. Our engineers will confirm the final selection against your glass size, wind load and fixing details.

Material Grades

  • Cast stainless steel (spiders): CF8, CF8M (ASTM A743/A743M). Optional CD3MN (ASTM A890/A890M). Non-standard grades similar to CF8/CF8M on request.
  • Stainless steel sheet, tube and bar: 304, 316 (ASTM A276/A276M, A666, A269/A269M). Optional 2205. Non-standard grades similar to 304/316 on request.
  • Standard: point-fixed devices for construction curtain walls to GB/T 37266-2018.

Austenitic 316 / CF8M is the most widely used grade for point-fixed fittings. It is not recommended for severe environments such as swimming pools or seaside sites; select duplex 2205 / CD3MN there.

Why Choose Duplex 2205 / CD3MN

  • Yield strength about twice that of common austenitic stainless steel, allowing smaller, more slender fittings.
  • Higher material utilisation, which saves material.
  • Excellent resistance to pitting and intergranular corrosion in harsh surroundings.
  • Superior resistance to stress corrosion cracking, including in chloride and seaside environments.
  • Thermal expansion close to carbon steel and reinforced concrete, so low thermal stress.
  • No pre-heating or post-weld heat treatment needed; it can be welded to carbon steel.

Care & Handling

Heat-treat after casting as required, choose the grade for the environment, specify a smooth surface treatment, protect the fittings during installation, avoid fouling and water accumulation, and clean regularly.

Fx is the axial load and Fy the radial load on a single arm, in newtons. A model is the series number plus the arm code, for example A160A4 or A160A21.

When ordering fin spiders, state whether the rubber washer or nylon sleeve is to be removed, and drill the glass to the bolt dimension.

1. Glass Panel Weight

Gk = T × B × H × ρ

  • Gk weight of the glass panel (N)
  • T valid glass thickness (mm)
  • B width of the glass (m)
  • H height of the glass (m)
  • ρ gravity density of glass (default 25.6)

2. Radial Force

Fy = 1.2 Gk / ny

  • Fy radial force on a single point (N)
  • Gk weight of the glass panel (N)
  • ny number of force-bearing points in the y direction

3. Axial Force

Fx = q × B × H / nx

  • Fx axial force on a single point (N)
  • q distributed design load on the glass, mainly wind load (N/m²)
  • B, H width and height of the glass (m)
  • nx number of force-bearing points in the x direction

For the standard four-point spider arrangement, nx = 4 and ny = 2. Select a spider whose recommended Fx and Fy are not lower than the calculated values.

Selection Guide

How to Specify a Spider Fitting

STEP 01
Calculate the Loads

Work out Fx and Fy for each fixing point from glass size, thickness and wind load.

STEP 02
Pick Series & Arm Code

Choose a series that meets the recommended load, then add the arm code, e.g. A160A4 or 220B21.

STEP 03
Select Material & Finish

CF8 / CF8M or CD3MN, in mirror, satin, sandblasted or plated finish to suit the environment.

STEP 04
Confirm Drilling Details

Drill the glass to the bolt dimension and state if the rubber washer or nylon sleeve is to be removed.

Applications

Where Spider Fittings Are Used

From single-layer cable-net walls to cable-truss, rod-truss and steel-fin systems, spiders carry the glass without frames or mullions.
